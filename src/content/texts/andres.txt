# Castilian Spanish (Spain), B1. Tenses on purpose:
#   present for his situation now (paragraphs 1–2), with the subjunctive
#     ("un trabajo que le emocione", "no hay ni uno que le emocione")
#   past for what happened (3–4): imperfecto for background and feelings
#     ("mientras tomaba un café", "le daba miedo") against indefinido for
#     completed events ("le contó", "le dio vueltas", "se armó de valor")
#   pretérito perfecto for today ("esta mañana ha decidido")
#   future for his plans (5): "seguirá", "dedicará", "saldrá"
# Synonyms on purpose: empresa / negocio, curro / trabajo,
# lanzarse a la piscina / dar el paso.
